### F1: Media-services reset turns a failed restore into reset recovery  (skipped)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** skipped
- **location:** PodHaven/Play/PlayManager+OnDeckRecovery.swift:36 (scope: in-diff)
- **concern:** `beginMediaServicesRecovery` always records `.mediaServicesReset`, so a reset while a failed restore awaits its retry makes that retry keep the in-memory play rate (1.0 after launch) instead of the podcast or global default.
- **resolution:** Needs a mediaserverd reset inside the failed-restore wait; the harm is one playback at the wrong speed that the user can change. Preserving the cause adds a branch and a reset test for a rare, self-limiting case.
