### F1: Cleanup keeps a removed checkout's cache forever once its packages became private  (fixed)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** bin/_worktrees.py:122 `artifact_references` (scope: in-diff)
- **concern:** The seed's `workspace-state.json` keeps paths a checkout recorded through its link, as it now does for the primary's DerivedData.
