### F1: Uptime gaps hide device sleep  (fixed)

- **severity:** Nit
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** docs/sentry-recovered-hang-diagnostics.md:111
- **concern:** `ProcessInfo.systemUptime` counts only awake time, so `secondsBeforeCapture` understates the age of a pre-sleep milestone such as `launched`, and the guide gave no caveat.
- **resolution:** Guide now says to use uptime within the hang window and timestamps for longer gaps.
- **fixed_at:** 2026-10-28

### F2: Startup-marker thread flag misread  (fixed)

- **severity:** Nit
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** docs/sentry-recovered-hang-diagnostics.md:100
- **concern:** The guide treated main-thread startup markers as our startup work, but every `startup.*` marker is recorded from the `AsyncOnce` task, so its flag shows the recording thread, not where awaited work ran.
- **resolution:** Guide now explains the flag and reads overlapping startup markers as contention or awaited main-actor work.
- **fixed_at:** 2026-10-28
