### F1: A route loss AVPlayer plays through claims a later pause  (fixed)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** fixed
- **scope:** in-diff
- **location:** PodHaven/Play/PlayManager+RouteChangeRecovery.swift:159
- **concern:** `.awaitingPause` stayed armed after a loss that left AVPlayer playing, so the next unrequested pause under that request, such as AVPlayer's own pause before an interruption notification, counted as a route-loss pause and the interruption didn't resume.
